\section{Implementation details}
\label{sec:impl}

\paragraph{Token interfaces.}
\Cref{tab:tokens} lists the interfaces of the model.
Each target window contains eight frames at positions 4--11 of a 17-frame video context.
HMR\,2.0a supplies a 192-token image grid per frame; each of the five VACE latent slices is pooled from a $6\times6$ grid to $2\times3$ cells, giving 30 memory tokens.
The dual-order decoder has width 256 and eight heads, with four blocks in each of three weight-shared iterations.
In the refiner, local image samples are projected to 64 channels and combined with the 256-dimensional pose query and the 22-dimensional body state (9 rotation, 10 shape, 3 camera values per joint) into a 128-dimensional joint feature.

\paragraph{Router tensors.}
For a batch of $B$ windows, a source is given by residuals and projections $[B,T,17,2]$, the log-confidence feature and the confidence $[B,T,17,1]$, frame validity $[B,T]$ and slot validity $[B,T,17]$.
For external keypoints $(u,v,c)$, the log-confidence feature is $-2\log\max(c,0.0025)$ clipped to $[-6,6]$, the confidence feature is $c$, and a slot is valid when it is finite and $c$ exceeds a minimum confidence (zero in all reported runs); frames with a non-positive or non-finite camera scale are invalid.
The token MLP is $\mathrm{Linear}(7,128)$--SiLU--$\mathrm{Linear}(128,128)$, the encoded tokens have shape $[BT,17,128]$, the 24 joint queries $[BT,24,128]$, and the additive attention mask $[4BT,24,17]$ holds the bias $-D(j,a(l))/2$ and $-\infty$ for invalid slots.
If no slot of a frame is valid, slot~1 is temporarily unmasked to keep the softmax finite and the output of that frame is set to zero.
The output $\mathrm{Linear}(128,128)$ without bias is zero-initialised and yields $[B,T,24,128]$, added to the joint features at every refinement iteration.
The anchors $a(l)$ map the five COCO facial landmarks to the SMPL head joint and shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees and ankles to the corresponding SMPL joints; $D$ is the shortest-path distance on the 24-joint SMPL tree.
Variant \vB{5} permutes the 17 bias columns with the fixed derangement $(9,13,5,16,11,2,15,0,12,4,14,1,8,6,3,10,7)$ (zero-based), which has no fixed point.
The router of each run is initialised on the CPU from its own generator with seed $370026+(s-2026)$ for training seed $s$, so that it does not change the random stream of the rest of the model; for $s=2026$ this is the initialisation of the archived C10 router.

For checkpoint compatibility with C10, the module always instantiates the full C10 parameter set; tensors that a configuration does not use receive no gradient and are never updated (``instantiated'' column).

\paragraph{External keypoints.}
ViTPose++-H (COCO-17 expert head, transformers implementation) runs top-down on the central $192\times256$ region of the same $256\times256$ crop that the HMR\,2.0a backbone sees; coordinates are normalised as $x/256-0.5$, the convention of the body projection.
The keypoints are cached once per split and never fine-tuned.
For \vC{1} and \vC{4}, the OpenPose-18 detections distributed with 3DPW (the \texttt{poses2d} field) are mapped to COCO-17 with their confidences; these are the same detections that define the crop box.
No ground-truth 2D or 3D joint enters either cache.

\paragraph{Bounded rotation updates.}
Write $w=\tanh u\in(-1,1)^3$. Then $\lVert w\rVert<\sqrt3$ and, since $s\mapsto s/\sqrt{1+s^2}$ is increasing, $\lVert\omega\rVert=\alpha\,\lVert w\rVert/\sqrt{1+\lVert w\rVert^2}<\alpha\sqrt3/2$.
With $\alpha=2^\circ$ (root) and $5^\circ$ (other joints) each step is below $1.73^\circ$ and $4.33^\circ$, and by the triangle inequality for the geodesic distance on $SO(3)$ three steps move a joint by less than $5.2^\circ$ and $13.0^\circ$ relative to the refiner input.
This bounds the refinement displacement, not the error with respect to the ground truth.
The archived manuscript stated the looser bounds $6^\circ$ and $15^\circ$, which also hold.

\section{Objective and optimisation}
\label{sec:optim}

  \centering\small

    \midrule

    Rotation / shape error & 0.05 / 0.001 \\

    Shape consistency & 0.01 \\
    Update regularisation & 0.001 \\
    2D reprojection, observation and visibility terms & 0 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{Loss coefficients, identical for all variants. Coordinate losses are in metres; the two acceleration coefficients implement the count-weighted within/boundary partition and sum to four.}
  \label{tab:loss}
\end{table}

\Cref{tab:loss} lists the loss coefficients.
Joint and vertex terms use Euclidean errors; rotation and shape supervision use squared errors.
Velocity and acceleration act on the hip-centred prediction-minus-target joint error $\epsilon_t$; at an annotation-adjacent triplet the acceleration error is $\frac{1}{14}\sum_{j=1}^{14}\lVert\epsilon_{t-1,j}-2\epsilon_{t,j}+\epsilon_{t+1,j}\rVert_2$.
Every person-frame is owned by one window; triplets inside an owner window and triplets across owner windows form disjoint sets, and the boundary set is traversed cyclically with its owner contexts forwarded separately, so that the final-output motion objective covers complete sequences.
All 2D terms have weight zero; this is why the inherited observation head never received gradient (\cref{sec:audit}).

Every variant starts from the same parent and runs 1,000 optimiser steps with AdamW (weight decay $10^{-4}$ on matrices), gradient-norm clipping at one and an effective batch of 16 windows (batch one, 16 accumulation steps), which amounts to 16,000 training-window draws and 16,000 boundary-triplet draws, covering all 2,909 training windows and all 5,548 boundary triplets.
Neural modules run under BF16 autocast and geometry in FP32.
All parameters outside the decoder backbone (track-shape residual, recurrent refiner with its part/time blocks, and the feedback module) train at $5\times10^{-5}$ from step zero; at step 300 the body, root and shape heads and the output normalisation become trainable at $5\times10^{-6}$, and at step 800 the last two decoder blocks, the image attention and the inherited feedback projection at $10^{-6}$.
The reference, feature encoders, camera head and observation head stay frozen.
Learning rates use 200 warm-up steps and a cosine schedule with a 6,000-step horizon and a 10\% floor; at update $k$ the EMA decay is $\min(0.999,(1+k)/(10+k))$.
The evaluated weights are the EMA at step 1,000 for every variant and seed.

\section{Evaluation protocol and deviations}
\label{sec:protocol}

\paragraph{Metrics.}
Predicted bodies use the neutral SMPL model and targets the gendered SMPL model.
Joints are the 14 LSP joints obtained from mesh vertices with the H36M regressor.
\texttt{mpjpe} subtracts the H36M pelvis (joint~0) from both skeletons, as in SPIN, CameraHMR and ReFit; \texttt{mpjpehip} subtracts the midpoint of the two LSP hips, as in the VIBE evaluator, WHAM and GVHMR.
\texttt{pampjpe} applies a per-frame similarity Procrustes alignment.
\texttt{pve} is our historical vertex error without any alignment, \texttt{pvepelvis} subtracts each body's H36M pelvis from its vertices (CameraHMR, ReFit, PromptHMR), and \texttt{pvehip} subtracts each body's LSP hip midpoint (WHAM, GVHMR).
VIBE, TCMR and GLoT compute PVE against neutral-gender ground-truth bodies without alignment, which we do not reproduce.
\texttt{accel} is the mean over all annotation-adjacent triplets (including triplets across inference windows) of the second difference of the hip-centred prediction-minus-target error, in mm/frame$^2$; \texttt{accelms} $=0.9\times$\texttt{accel} is the same quantity in m/s$^2$ at 30\,fps.
Aggregates weight person-frames (or triplets) equally; sequence errors are summed and divided by the total count.
Inference and SMPL computation run in FP32 with double-precision sums, without flip averaging or smoothing.

\paragraph{Deviations from detector-and-tracker pipelines.}
(i)~Person crops are derived from the 2D person annotations of 3DPW with a 25\% margin rather than from a person detector.
(ii)~Track identities are the dataset's person identities; the track prototype used by the shape residual is computed offline from the whole track and is therefore non-causal.
(iii)~Frame validity masks follow the dataset's camera-pose validity flags.
(iv)~Earlier models of this project were evaluated on the 3DPW test split before this study (we cannot undo that exposure); in this study, test evaluation is gated by validation locks and no decision used test results.
(v)~The historical unaligned PVE of earlier drafts is not comparable to recent literature; we report it only for continuity.
(vi)~The archived C10 numbers were selected on validation, the split they are reported on.
These deviations apply equally to all variants of the controlled study.

\section{Statistical procedure}
\label{sec:stats}

For variant $V$, control $C$, metric $m$, paired seeds $s\in\mathcal S$ and sequences $q\in\mathcal Q$ with counts $n_q$ (person-frames, or triplets for acceleration), let $E^{V}_{s,q}$ be the summed error of sequence $q$.
The reported difference is
\begin{equation}
  \Delta = \frac{\frac{1}{|\mathcal S|}\sum_{s}\sum_{q}\big(E^{V}_{s,q}-E^{C}_{s,q}\big)}{\sum_{q} n_q},
\end{equation}
\ie the frame-weighted aggregate difference averaged over seeds.
Let $\Delta_s$ be the aggregate difference of seed $s$, so that $\Delta$ is their mean.
The interval is $\Delta \pm t_{|\mathcal S|-1,\,0.975}\,\sqrt{\sigma^2_{\text{seed}}/|\mathcal S| + \sigma^2_{\text{seq}}}$, where $\sigma^2_{\text{seed}}$ is the sample variance of the $\Delta_s$ and $\sigma^2_{\text{seq}}$ is the variance of $\Delta$ under a cluster bootstrap that resamples $|\mathcal Q|$ sequences with replacement from the seed-averaged sequence differences (20,000 resamples, random seed 20261101~\cite{efron1993bootstrap}); the $p$-value uses the same statistic and degrees of freedom.
The first term captures training variance, the second the sampling of test sequences; with three seeds the $t$ quantile is $4.30$, which keeps coverage honest at the price of wide intervals.
A percentile bootstrap that resamples seeds and then sequences is also stored in \texttt{evidence/c11/summary.json}, but it is too narrow with three seeds and is not used for any claim.
``Wins'' counts sequences whose seed-averaged difference is negative.
We call a difference reliable only if its interval excludes zero and all three seeds agree in sign; this rule and the list of comparisons were fixed before test evaluation.
We do not correct for the 14 comparisons; the two primary ones are \vB{4} \vs \vB{1} and \vB{4} \vs \vB{7}.

\section{Observation-head audit and archived records}
\label{sec:audit}

\paragraph{Audit.}
The parent's observation head predicts landmark coordinates as a correction to the projection of the frozen reference body, a log-variance and a visibility logit.
It is created with zero coordinate and log-variance weights, log-variance bias $-4$ and zero visibility weights.
In every training stage that produced the parent, C00 and C10, all 2D losses have weight zero (\cref{tab:loss}), and the stage contracts forbid enabling them; the head therefore receives no gradient.
If the head is still at its initialisation, its coordinates equal the reference projection, its log-variance is $-4$ and its visibility is $\sigma(0)=0.5$ for every input, and the residual routed by C10 is $p(x^{\text{ref}})-p(x^r)$.
\texttt{scripts/inspect\_checkpoint\_v43.py} checks these conditions on the stored weights and EMA weights of each checkpoint.
